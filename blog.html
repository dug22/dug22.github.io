Understanding The Perceptron Model

This blog provides an in-depth exploration of the fundamental steps involved in Rosenblatt's Perceptron Model. The perceptron serves as a fundamental unit of logic in neural networks. The perceptron is modified to handle binary classifying tasks, distinguising between two possible outcomes (1 or 0, yes or no, etc.).
